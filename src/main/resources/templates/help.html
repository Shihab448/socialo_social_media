Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common Socialo questions.

How do I change my password?

Go to Settings → Change Password, enter your current password, then choose a new secure password and save.

Why can't I send messages?

Make sure your internet connection is active and the other user has not blocked you. Try refreshing the messenger page.

How do I report a fake account?

Open the user's profile, tap the three-dot menu and select Report, or use the Report / Complaint page from the menu.

Can I edit or delete a post later?

Yes. Open the post, tap the three-dot menu (⋯) and choose Edit Post or Delete Post.
